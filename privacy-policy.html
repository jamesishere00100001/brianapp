Privacy Policy

Brian for iOS

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

Brian helps you look after your pets. This policy explains what information the Brian app for iPhone and iPad uses, where it is kept and how you can delete it. Brian is made by James Attersley, an independent developer.

In short: your pet information stays on your device and, if you sign in, in your own iCloud account. We don't run servers that store your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you or sell anything about you.

Information you add about your pets

This includes pet profiles, photos, care routines, medications, health and weight records, vet and insurance details, and documents you scan or import. It is used only to provide the app's features.

  • Guest mode: everything is stored only on your device.
  • Signed in: everything is stored on your device and synced through Apple's iCloud to the private iCloud database of the Apple Account signed in on your device. Apple stores it under its own privacy policy. We cannot see or access it.

Signing in

  • Sign in with Apple: Apple gives the app a unique user identifier, and your name and email address if you choose to share them (you can hide your email). These are stored only on your device. The identifier is kept in the device Keychain.
  • Sign in with Google: Google gives the app your Google account ID, name and email address. These are stored only on your device. Google processes the sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy.

We use this information only to keep you signed in and to show your name in the app.

Photos, camera and files

Brian only sees the photos you pick, the documents you scan with the camera and the files you import. They are stored with your pet's information as described above. If you save a photo from Brian, it is added to your photo library with your permission.

Reminders

Care reminders are scheduled as notifications on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Siri, Shortcuts and Apple Intelligence

If you log activities with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes the request under its own privacy policy. On supported devices, Brian's weekly recap is written by Apple's on-device language model. Your pet information is not sent to us or to any other service for this.

Subscriptions

Brian Plus is purchased and managed by Apple through the App Store. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription. We never receive your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.

If you choose to share a document or report from Brian (for example with your vet), it is sent using the iOS share sheet to whoever you choose.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your pets and records from your device and from your iCloud data for Brian, cancels scheduled reminders and, if you used Google, disconnects Brian from your Google account. Deleting the app removes data stored on the device. You can also stop Brian using iCloud in your device's iCloud settings. To revoke Sign in with Apple, go to Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple. Subscriptions are cancelled in your App Store account settings.

Children

Brian is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below.
